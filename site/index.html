Why now

The rules changed in 2024–2025

The UAE is the world's second-largest gold hub, and its AML regime was rebuilt almost overnight. Thousands of mid-sized dealers just inherited a heavy, audited obligation — with nothing built to handle it.

01

Decree 68 of 2024

Responsible-sourcing due diligence extended beyond refiners to everyone who trades, recycles or handles gold.

02

Federal Decree-Law 10/2025

With Cabinet Resolution 134/2025, this sets the current AML/CFT obligations for the whole sector.

03

2024 National Risk Assessment

Officially classifies precious-metals dealers as medium-to-high money-laundering risk.

04

Active enforcement

The Ministry of Economy & Tourism is inspecting and issuing fines — 32 refinery licences suspended in one 2024 sweep.

Product · how it works

Four AI engines, one compliance pipeline

Every transaction flows through one guided process — the dealer sees a simple workflow; the obligation is handled underneath.

Automated KYC / CDD

AI extraction from Emirates IDs, passports and trade licences. Sanctions and PEP screening, with each customer risk-scored against the UAE FIU red-flag indicators.

Transaction monitoring

Flags the AED 55,000 threshold — including the linked transactions dealers routinely miss — and auto-drafts the suspicious-transaction narrative.

Responsible-sourcing due diligence

Guides the dealer through the OECD five-step process, ingests supplier documents, and generates the audit-ready risk assessment Decree 68 now demands.

goAML report generation

Turns all of the above into a filing-ready report in the required goAML format. A compliance officer reviews and files — nothing is submitted automatically.

Built & live

Defensible by design

A type-safe TypeScript platform — Next.js web, NestJS API, PostgreSQL — with AI adapters for Arabic-ID OCR, sanctions/PEP screening and narrative generation, and pure compliance engines for the AED 55k threshold, linked-transaction aggregation and goAML XML.

  • ✓Per-dealer isolation enforced in the database — row-level security, not app code, keeps one dealer's data invisible to another.
  • ✓Tamper-evident audit log — hash-chained (SHA-256); historical records cannot be altered or deleted.
  • ✓Encrypted & private — documents encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM); no personal data in logs.
  • ✓A human files every report — the flow always ends at a human review-and-file step; never automated.
